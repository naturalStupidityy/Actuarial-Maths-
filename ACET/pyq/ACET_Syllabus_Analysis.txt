================================================================================
                           CLASS 12 MATHEMATICS
================================================================================

1. RELATIONS AND FUNCTIONS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in 20+ papers)
   Application: Domain, range, function equality, composite functions, inverse 
                functions, types of functions (injective, surjective, bijective)

2. INVERSE TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 15+ papers)
   Application: Direct questions on principal values, identities involving 
                inverse trig functions, simplification using inverse trig

3. MATRICES
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in 22+ papers)
   Application: Matrix operations, transpose, inverse, rank of matrix, solving 
                systems of equations, adjoint, determinant properties, matrix 
                equations, special matrices (symmetric, skew-symmetric)

4. DETERMINANTS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in 20+ papers)
   Application: Evaluating determinants, properties of determinants, area of 
                triangle, solving linear equations using Cramer's rule, 
                minors and cofactors

5. CONTINUITY AND DIFFERENTIABILITY
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in 22+ papers)
   Application: Checking continuity at a point, differentiability, finding 
                derivatives using chain rule, implicit differentiation, 
                parametric differentiation, second order derivatives

6. APPLICATION OF DERIVATIVES
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in 23+ papers)
   Application: Increasing/decreasing functions, maxima/minima (local and 
                global), tangents and normals, rate of change, optimization 
                problems, Rolle's theorem, Lagrange's mean value theorem

7. INTEGRALS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Very High (appears in all 24 papers)
   Application: Indefinite integrals, definite integrals, substitution method, 
                integration by parts, partial fractions, special integrals, 
                properties of definite integrals

8. APPLICATION OF INTEGRALS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 15+ papers)
   Application: Area under curves, area between curves, area bounded by 
                regions, volume calculations

9. DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Medium (appears in 10+ papers)
   Application: Order and degree of differential equations, solving first-order 
                differential equations, variable separable method, homogeneous 
                equations, linear differential equations

10. VECTOR ALGEBRA
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: Very High (appears in 22+ papers)
    Application: Vector operations, dot product, cross product, scalar triple 
                 product, vector triple product, angle between vectors, unit 
                 vectors, position vectors, section formula

11. THREE DIMENSIONAL GEOMETRY
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: High (appears in 15+ papers)
    Application: Direction cosines and direction ratios, equation of lines and 
                 planes in 3D, angle between lines/planes, distance from point 
                 to plane, shortest distance between skew lines

12. LINEAR PROGRAMMING
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: Medium (appears in 8+ papers)
    Application: Formulation of LPP, graphical method, feasible region, 
                 optimal solutions, corner point method

13. PROBABILITY (Class 12)
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: Very High (appears in all 24 papers)
    Application: Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, independent events, 
                 multiplication theorem, total probability theorem, random 
                 variables, probability distributions

================================================================================
                           CLASS 11 MATHEMATICS
================================================================================

1. SETS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 18+ papers)
   Application: Set operations, Venn diagrams, union, intersection, complement, 
                set builder notation, power sets, subsets

2. RELATIONS AND FUNCTIONS (Class 11)
   Status: Prerequisite
   Frequency: Very High (used in all 24 papers)
   Application: Cartesian product, types of relations (reflexive, symmetric, 
                transitive), equivalence relations, one-one and onto functions, 
                composition of functions

3. TRIGONOMETRIC FUNCTIONS
   Status: Direct + Prerequisite
   Frequency: Very High (appears in all 24 papers)
   Application: Direct questions on trig identities, complementary angles, 
                periodicity; PREREQUISITE for calculus (integration of trig 
                functions, derivatives of trig functions)

4. COMPLEX NUMBERS
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 16+ papers)
   Application: Modulus, argument, conjugate, polar form, Euler's form, 
                operations with complex numbers, De Moivre's theorem

5. LINEAR INEQUALITIES
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Medium (appears in 10+ papers)
   Application: Solving linear inequalities, graphical representation of 
                inequalities, system of linear inequalities

6. PERMUTATIONS AND COMBINATIONS
   Status: Direct + Prerequisite
   Frequency: Very High (appears in all 24 papers)
   Application: Direct counting problems, arrangement problems, selection 
                problems; PREREQUISITE for probability calculations, binomial 
                theorem expansions

7. BINOMIAL THEOREM
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 15+ papers)
   Application: Expansion of binomial expressions, general term, middle term, 
                binomial coefficients, properties of binomial coefficients, 
                approximations using binomial theorem

8. SEQUENCES AND SERIES
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: High (appears in 18+ papers)
   Application: Arithmetic Progression (AP), Geometric Progression (GP), 
                Harmonic Progression (HP), arithmetic mean, geometric mean, 
                sum to n terms, sum of infinite GP, special series

9. STRAIGHT LINES
   Status: Direct
   Frequency: Medium (appears in 12+ papers)
   Application: Slope of a line, various forms of line equations, angle between 
                lines, distance from point to line, distance between parallel 
                lines, family of lines

10. CONIC SECTIONS
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: Medium (appears in 10+ papers)
    Application: Circle (equation, tangent, normal), parabola, ellipse, 
                 hyperbola - standard equations and properties

11. INTRODUCTION TO THREE DIMENSIONAL GEOMETRY
    Status: Prerequisite
    Frequency: High (used in 18+ papers)
    Application: Distance formula in 3D, section formula, coordinates of points 
                 in 3D space - PREREQUISITE for Class 12 3D geometry

12. LIMITS AND DERIVATIVES
    Status: Direct + Prerequisite
    Frequency: Very High (appears in all 24 papers)
    Application: Direct limit evaluation questions, algebra of limits, standard 
                 limits, derivatives using first principle; PREREQUISITE for 
                 calculus (differentiation, integration concepts)

13. MATHEMATICAL REASONING
    Status: Direct
    Frequency: Low (appears in 5+ papers)
    Application: Statements, logical connectives, negation, contrapositive, 
                 converse, validity of statements

14. STATISTICS (Class 11)
    Status: Prerequisite
    Frequency: High (used in 20+ papers)
    Application: Measures of dispersion, mean deviation, variance, standard 
                 deviation - PREREQUISITE for advanced statistics

15. PROBABILITY (Class 11)
    Status: Prerequisite
    Frequency: Very High (used in all 24 papers)
    Application: Basic probability concepts, sample space, events, mutually 
                 exclusive events, exhaustive events - PREREQUISITE for Class 12 
                 probability

   Status: Direct | Frequency: Very High
   Application: Binomial distribution, Poisson distribution, Normal distribution, 
                their properties, mean, variance, probability calculations

                expected value, variance, moments
